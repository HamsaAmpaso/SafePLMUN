About SafePLMUN

SafePLMUN is a real time emergency and safety response system that keeps users prepared, and supported when it matters most.

About SafePLMUN

SafePLMUN is a campus safety and emergency response system for Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Muntinlupa. It enables users to report emergencies such as violence, fires, earthquakes, and medical emergencies. When an emergency is reported real-time alerts, safety instructions, and evacuation information are sent to the campus community. SafePLMUN helps connect users and the school during emergencies to create a safer and more prepared campus.

SafePLMUN Priorities

Earthquake

Provide real time alert, safety actions to conduct, and possible evacuation area after the earthquake has been reported.

Fire

Provide real time alert, location of fire, safety actions to conduct, and possible evacuation area after the fire has been reported.

Violence

Provide real time alert, location where the violent incident is taking place, safety actions to conduct, and possible evacuation area after the violent incident has been reported.

Injuries

Provide real time alert to the school clinic so they can quickly respond to the reported medical emergency.

Our Campus

SafePLMUN's goal is a safer campus where every student knows where to turn when it matters most.

Step onto campus knowing help is never far away. SafePLMUN puts safety information, emergency resources, and support within reach helping you report incidents, request medical assistance, and look out for those who matter.

Our Features

SafePLMUN offers useful and interactive features to ensure safety and connectivity when it matters most.

Real Time Report Based Alert System

SafePLMUN allows users to report earthquakes, fires, and school violence. The system alerts all users about the incident, providing safety actions and possible evacuation areas.

Medical Emergency Reporting System

SafePLMUN allows users to remotely report medical emergencies to the campus clinic. The clinic has a dedicated SafePLMUN account to receive requests and respond quickly when help is needed.

User Emergency Status Check System

After SafePLMUN alerts users about a reported incident the users can mark themselves safe, or in need of help to communicate their status during an emergency.

Interactive Buddy System

SafePLMUN allows users to add another user as their buddy, when two users are buddy when there is a reported emergency they can see each other's current status. Allowing them to be more connected when it matters most.

False Alarm Reporting

SafePLMUN allows the admin to send out false alarm alert when a reported incident was proved to be false alarm. To ensure the integrity of SafePLMUN's real time emergency report and alert system.

              SafePLMUN Guidelines

              One Campus. One Community. One Priority: Safety.

              1. Report Emergencies Responsibly

              Use SafePLMUN to report genuine emergencies that require immediate attention, such as fire, violence, earthquakes, and medical emergencies.

              2. Provide Accurate Information

              Provide the correct emergency type and location whenever possible. Accurate information helps responders understand the situation and coordinate assistance.

              3. Follow Emergency Instructions

              Read emergency alerts carefully and follow instructions from authorized school personnel. Move to a designated safe area or evacuate when instructed.

              4. Keep Your Status Updated

              During an active emergency, use the available status options to indicate whether you are safe, need help, or need medical assistance.

              5. Do Not Put Yourself at Risk

              Do not investigate dangerous situations or attempt a rescue if doing so could put you or others in danger. Prioritize reaching a safe location and follow official instructions.

              6. Do Not Abuse the System

              Do not submit false reports, prank reports, spam, or misleading information. Misuse of the system can interfere with genuine emergency response.

              7. Respect Other Users

              Respect the privacy and safety of other users. Do not use SafePLMUN to harass, threaten, or spread unnecessary personal information.

              8. Stay Calm and Informed

              During an emergency, avoid spreading unverified information. Rely on official SafePLMUN alerts and instructions from authorized school personnel.

              By continuing, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these guidelines.
